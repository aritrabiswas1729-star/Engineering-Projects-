## 🔬 Research Direction

### Robust Control of Power-Electronic Converters for HESS-Based EV Applications

My primary research interest is the development of **robust control methodologies for power-electronic converters used in Hybrid Energy Storage Systems (HESS)** for electric vehicles.

My research interests include:

* Robust and optimal control of DC-DC converters
* H∞ and LPV-based control
* Uncertainty modeling and analysis
* Weighting-function selection
* Loop-shaping techniques
* Bidirectional converter control
* Battery–supercapacitor power management
* DC-bus voltage regulation
* Regenerative-braking applications
* MATLAB/Simulink-based validation

> **Research Question:**
> How can advanced control techniques maintain stability and performance of power-electronic converters despite parameter variations, uncertain operating conditions, and rapidly changing EV power demands?

## 🚀 Selected Technical Work

### 01 — Robust Control for HESS in Electric Vehicles

**Research / M.Tech Direction**

Investigation of robust control techniques for bidirectional DC-DC converters interfacing batteries and supercapacitors in EV hybrid energy-storage architectures.

**Focus**

`H∞ Control` · `LPV Modeling` · `Loop Shaping` · `Uncertainty` · `HESS` · `EV`

**Key Objectives**

* Maintain DC-bus voltage regulation
* Handle converter parameter variations
* Separate low- and high-frequency power components
* Improve robustness against uncertain operating conditions
* Validate controller performance through simulation

---

### 02 — Leader-Following Consensus of Heterogeneous DC-DC Converters

**Control Systems Research / Simulation**

Adaptation and simulation of a leader-following consensus framework for **heterogeneous DC-DC converter networks**, with emphasis on mixed **Negative Imaginary (NI) + Positive Real (PR)** control concepts.

**Technical Components**

* Heterogeneous converter modeling
* Leader-following consensus
* Graph-based communication
* Negative Imaginary (NI) theory
* Positive Real (PR) systems
* Distributed control
* MATLAB simulation
